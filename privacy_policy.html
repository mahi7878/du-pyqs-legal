PYQS ("the App", "we", "us", "our") helps Delhi University students find and read previous year question papers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

PYQS is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the University of Delhi.

1Information we collect#

1.1Information you provide

  • Account details: your email address and password when you sign up with email, or the basic profile details Google shares with us (such as name, email address and profile picture) when you use Google Sign-In. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored in hashed form; we cannot see your password.
  • Profile details: your name and the course you select.
  • App preferences: theme, notification setting, and your default course and semester.

1.2Information created when you use the App

  • Bookmarks: the papers you save.
  • Download records: which papers you downloaded and the status of the download.
  • Recently viewed papers.
  • In-app notifications sent to your account and whether you have read them.
  • Membership: if you subscribe to DU PYQS Exclusive, your subscription status, renewal and expiry dates, and the Google Play purchase token we use to confirm your subscription with Google Play. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never receive your card, UPI or bank details.

1.3Information stored on your device

The App stores some data locally on your device, such as cached paper lists, a copy of your saved-papers list (so it is still there after you restart the App or when you are offline), downloaded PDF files, your last-read page, recent searches and preferences. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

Papers you download are stored in the App's private storage on your device, are opened only inside the App, and can be deleted by you at any time from the Downloads tab. While a paper is open, the App asks Android to block screenshots and screen recording; this is a device setting applied by the App and it does not collect or send any screen content.

1.4Technical information

Our hosting and service providers may automatically process technical data such as IP address, device and operating-system type, and request timestamps as part of normal server operation, security and fraud prevention.

1.5Advertising data (free users)

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Premium (Exclusive) members do not see ads and the ad service is not loaded for them. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). We do not give Google or any advertiser your name, email address, course or other account details.

1.6What we do not collect

The App does not ask for your precise or approximate location, contacts, SMS, camera or microphone, and we do not collect or store your payment card or bank details (payments are handled by Google Play).

2How we use your information#

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide the core features of the App: showing papers, saving bookmarks, download history and recently viewed papers, and syncing them across your devices.
  • To remember your preferences and default course.
  • To send in-app notifications about the App or its content.
  • To confirm and manage your DU PYQS Exclusive subscription and unlock premium content.
  • To show ads to free users through Google AdMob.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, secure and improve the App.
  • To respond to your emails, copyright complaints and support requests.
  • To comply with applicable law and protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your account details (name, email, course) to target ads or to build advertising profiles.

3Consent#

We process your personal information based on your consent, which you give when you create an account and use the App, and where needed to provide the service you asked for or to meet legal obligations. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account (see Section 8). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before withdrawal.

4Who we share information with#

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the App, and only as needed to provide the service:

  • Supabase (supabase.com/privacy): authentication, database and file storage for your account data and App content.
  • Google LLC (policies.google.com/privacy): Google Sign-In and Google Play Services, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Backblaze, Inc. (backblaze.com/company/privacy.html): storage and delivery of paper PDF files. When you open or download a paper, your device connects to this provider, which can see technical data such as your IP address.
  • Google Play Billing (policies.google.com/privacy): processes subscription payments and tells us whether your subscription is valid.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads (policies.google.com/technologies/ads): shows ads to free users and may collect advertising ID and device data as described in Section 1.5.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order or a lawful request from a government authority, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or the public. If the App is ever transferred to another owner, your information may be transferred with it, and this Policy will continue to apply unless you are notified of changes. We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

5Where your data is stored#

Our service providers may store and process data on servers located outside India. We choose reputable providers that use industry-standard safeguards, but by using the App you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

6How long we keep information#

We keep your account and activity data for as long as your account is active. Subscription records are kept as long as needed to run your membership and meet legal and accounting needs. When you delete your account, we delete your data as described in Section 8. Some copies may remain in routine backups for a limited period, or be kept longer where the law requires. Data stored on your device is under your control and is removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it.

7Your rights and choices#

You can:

  • Update your name and course in your profile and your preferences in Settings.
  • Remove bookmarks, clear recently viewed items, and delete downloaded files inside the App.
  • Turn notifications off in Settings and sign out at any time.
  • Cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play, and opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • Withdraw your consent and delete your account from the Profile screen (Section 8).
  • Make a complaint about how we handle your data (Section 14).

To exercise these rights, email us at createstartup404@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. If you are in India, you also have the rights given to data principals under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8Account and data deletion#

You can permanently delete your PYQS account and the data linked to it at any time, from inside the App.

  • How to delete: go to Profile > Delete Account and confirm. For your security the App asks you to confirm your identity again first (your password, or your Google account).
  • What happens: the deletion is carried out on our servers through a secure process. It is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • What is deleted: your authentication account (email and sign-in details), profile (name and course), settings, saved papers, download records, recently viewed papers, notifications and subscription records.
  • On your device: after a successful deletion the App also removes the papers you downloaded to your device and the cached data and preferences it kept for your account, and signs you out.
  • Other ways to ask: you can also email createstartup404@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my PYQS account". We will confirm your identity and delete your account within 30 days. If you delete by email, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App to remove files stored on your device.
  • Important: deleting your PYQS account does NOT cancel a Google Play subscription. If you ever subscribed, cancel it yourself in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, or you may continue to be charged by Google.
  • What may remain: copies in routine backups of our service providers until they are overwritten, and information we must keep to comply with law. We keep nothing else.

9Security#

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access controls on our database, and password hashing by our authentication provider. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details safe.

10Children's privacy#

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Users under 18 should use the App with the knowledge and consent of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

11Content ownership and copyright#

The previous year question papers available in the App are made available for educational purposes to help students prepare for their exams. Ownership and copyright in these papers remain with their original owners, such as the University of Delhi and the respective authors. We do not claim ownership of any of them.

If you believe any file in the App infringes your copyright or other rights, please email us at createstartup404@gmail.com with the name of the paper, a description of the work you own, and your contact details. We will review the request and remove the content promptly, and in any case within 72 hours of verifying your notice.

12Third-party services#

Third-party services listed in Section 4 have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. Please read their policies to understand how they handle your information.

13Changes to this policy#

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date, and for material changes we will also notify you in the App. Please review this page periodically. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated Policy.

14Contact us and grievance redressal#

If you have any questions, requests or complaints about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact the PYQS Grievance Officer at createstartup404@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge your message within 7 days and resolve it within 30 days.
